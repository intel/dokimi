### Fixed

- **Statistics of finite values near the float range stay finite or refuse** — `bootstrap_ci` (and `mean_ci` without SciPy) reports the mean, `lo` and `hi` of finite values whose sum overflows, such as `[1.7e308] * 3`, instead of `inf`. `paired_test`, `arm_significance`, `paired_summary` and `paired_delta` raise `OverflowError` when finite scores differ by more than the float maximum (`[1.7e308] * 4` against `[-1.7e308] * 4`), where they returned an `inf` delta mean, a `nan` spread and no `p`. `point_biserial` returns `r` `None` with the reason `a total is not finite` for an `inf`, `-inf` or `nan` total, where it returned `nan` with no reason, and reads totals that vary at a tiny scale, such as `1e-300` to `4e-300`, as a correlation instead of "no variance in the totals".
