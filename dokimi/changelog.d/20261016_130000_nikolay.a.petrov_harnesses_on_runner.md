### Changed

- **`command:`, Harbor, BenchFlow, explorer and `repo run` processes are contained** —
  these now start through the process runner, so a timeout kills the whole process
  tree (an agent's tool calls, `harbor`'s children), not only the command, and
  anything the command leaves running when it exits is killed too. The command's
  stdin is closed, and its output is read as UTF-8 whatever the locale. A timed-out
  `bench` or `docker run` gets SIGTERM and 15 s / 10 s to stop before SIGKILL, also
  on Ctrl-C (where `bench` used to be left running). `repo run`'s `docker build`
  fails after 3 h and `docker image inspect` after 60 s instead of hanging the
  batch. `bench.log` keeps the first and last 512 KiB of each of `bench`'s
  streams. A wrapper that relied on a background process outliving its cell must
  now wait for it.
