### Fixed

- **A reward beyond the float range no longer crashes `dokimi tasks` or the task summary.** An integer such as `10**400` in a task row's `reward` raised `OverflowError` in `summarize_task_results` and in the completion check. Such a row now counts as unscored, as a NaN or infinite reward already did: `dokimi tasks` reports the cell as unscored instead of stopping, and the summary leaves it out of `avg_reward`. The same integer in `elapsed_sec`, `metrics.operation_count` or `metrics.n_skill_invocations` no longer crashes the summary either. The per-repeat report renders non-finite and boolean rewards as `null`. The summary also keeps the average finite when the sum of finite rewards would overflow.
