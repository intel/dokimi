### Fixed

- **Means and standard deviations of finite values near the float maximum no longer overflow** — `point_biserial` reported "no variance in the totals" for totals such as `[1e308, 1e308, 1e308, -1e308]` because their sum overflowed, and the run-consistency coefficient of variation (`tool_calls_cv`, `turns_cv`, `output_tokens_cv`) overflowed or raised `OverflowError` for values near 1e154 and above. The totals and values are now scaled by a power of two first, so the result is the finite number the data give; a constant total still reports no variance, and ordinary inputs give the same results as before.
