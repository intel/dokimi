### Fixed

- **Means and standard deviations of finite values near the float maximum no longer overflow** — `point_biserial` reported "no variance in the totals" for totals such as `[1e308, 1e308, 1e308, -1e308]` because their sum overflowed, and the run-consistency coefficient of variation (`tool_calls_cv`, `turns_cv`, `output_tokens_cv`) overflowed or raised `OverflowError` for values near 1e154 and above. The totals and values are now scaled by a power of two first, so the result is the finite number the data give; a constant total still reports no variance. `point_biserial` gives the same results as before for ordinary totals. The coefficient of variation now goes through the shared standard deviation for every input: for ordinary values it can differ from before in the last digit (one ulp), and for finite values below about 1e-154, whose squares underflowed to a coefficient of 0.0, it now gives the exact value.
