### Fixed

- **`skill novelty analyze` keeps what a `--max-usd` stop paid for** — a run stopped by the cap (or by a call with no known price) used to exit with `nothing was written`. It now writes `<out stem>.partial.json` beside `novelty.json` (never over a finished file) with `status: "partial"`, the stage it reached, the extracted atoms, the spans and the probe answers and judgments so far, and exits non-zero naming the file and how a rerun with `DOKIMI_RESPONSE_CACHE` replays the paid calls at $0. `novelty view`, `--novelty-artifact` and every other reader of a `novelty.json` refuse a partial file; the `novelty.v1` schema accepts it. A record with `status: "partial"` and no `partial` block is refused too, by the `novelty.json` readers and by `novelty items` on a probe file, a stop on a call with no known price tells the user to price the model or drop `--max-usd` (a higher cap does not help it), and the partial record keeps the generated probe wordings in `probes.items`, also when the cap falls between the paraphrase call and the reversal call. The replay promise is withheld when the cache directory is not writable or a cache write failed during the run (a full disk), and `dokimi runs show` reports a finished `novelty.json` that lacks `paired`, `k0` or `spans` as invalid, as the loaders do. The partial record counts `atoms.n` as a finished one does (the probed sample), a stop in extraction keeps `run_config` (its warnings stay empty, and `unread_span_ids` names the spans not read), and `save_artifact("novelty", ...)` refuses a record with `status` or `partial`. `skill shorten` still writes nothing at the cap.
