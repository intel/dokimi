### Fixed

- **The `)` of a `case` pattern inside `$(...)` no longer closes the substitution for the heredoc opener scan** — in `echo "$(case x in x) printf '%s' "<<EOF";; esac)"` the pattern's `)` popped the `$(`, so the enclosing double quote was read as closed and the `<<EOF` in the inner string opened a heredoc; `shell.split_commands`, the heredoc strip of `repos/shell_walk.py` and the prefilter then lost every line after it (`cat AGENTS.md`). The quote stack of `text_spans.py` now tracks a `case` that starts a command inside a `$(` until its `esac`, so a pattern `)` pops nothing, and `(x)` patterns, nested cases and `esac` as an argument read as before. The prefilter's `_without_heredoc_bodies` also reads the quotes of a substitution as its own, so `echo "$(printf '%s' "<<EOF")"` opens no heredoc there either.
