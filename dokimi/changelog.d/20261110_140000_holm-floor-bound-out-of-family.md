### Changed

- **Holm's and BH's multiplier leaves out a result that cannot reach p < 0.05** — a result whose changed pairs give a sign-flip p floor `2 / 2^k` of 0.05 or more can never be significant, yet it counted in the multiplier of every other result in its family. It now stays out (`arms run` per arm and per interaction contrast, `report model-compare` per run, `repo compare` gate and score rows): a 6-pair run with p 0.0312 read `p_adjusted` 0.0625 and not significant next to a 2-pair run, and now reads 0.0312 and significant. A floor-bound result keeps its row, its `p` and a null `significant`; it still counts in `family_size`, `family_tested` counts only the results that can reach 0.05, and its `p_adjusted` (arms) or `p_holm` and `p_holm_floor` (`repo compare`) are `null`. `p_holm_floor` and `mde` describe the same smaller family, and the `repo compare` note on Holm reads `with Holm over N rows that can reach p < 0.05`. Verdicts near 0.05 move toward significant in families that hold a floor-bound result; a family without one reads as before.
