### Added

- **`catalog check` reports the shares per repo next to the shares per skill** — a catalog can hold one repo with thousands of skills, so the per-skill share is mostly that repo's. The per-repo share weights each repo equally (the mean over the repos of the share of its skills that have the state or code), and each repo counts once. A repo is the nearest directory up from a skill that holds a `.git` entry, under the scanned root. The text report adds the states and up to five codes ranked by the larger of the two shares, with both shares, when the scan has more than one repo; `--format json` carries them under a new top-level `rates` key, with or without `--per-skill`. Unchecked and skipped skills have separate state entries. An empty scan reports null shares.
