### Added

- **`catalog check` reports the shares per repo next to the shares per skill** — a catalog can hold one repo with thousands of skills, so the per-skill share is mostly that repo's. The per-repo share weights each repo equally (the mean over the repos of the share of its skills that have the state or code), and each repo counts once. A repo is the nearest directory up from a skill that holds a `.git` entry, under the scanned root. The text report adds the states and the five most common codes, with both shares, when the scan has more than one repo; `--format json` carries them under a new top-level `rates` key, with or without `--per-skill`.
