### Fixed

- **`paired_test` of finite deltas whose sum leaves the float range** — a row of deltas such as `[1e308 * (g / 9) for g in [2, 4, 3, 5, 6, 9, 7, 8]]` (each finite, their magnitudes summing to 4.9e308) was refused with an `OverflowError` for the sum of the magnitudes, though its mean (6.1e307), sd and sign-flip p all fit. `paired_test`, `arm_significance` and `interaction_test` now give the mean, sd, p and interval of such a row, and refuse a row only when its sd is beyond the float range (`[1.7e308, -1.7e308] * 3`), as before, naming the sd. `interaction_test` refuses a row whose two arm deltas add past the float range (its additive expectation would be `inf`). `model compare` sections keep refusing a sum past the range, naming the run.
