### Changed

- **Novelty's atom-clustered intervals come from the shared bootstrap.** `ci95_added` (the added rate and the noise floor), the placebo-gated `ci95` and the atom resampling in `novelty` now use `dokimi.eval.stats.bootstrap_statistics` with an integer seed, where they used a string-seeded stream of their own. The upper bound also takes the order statistic every other interval takes (index 389 of 400, where it was 390). The resample count (400) and the output shape are unchanged, but the bounds move, by Monte Carlo noise and the index. Measured on 2,000 random inputs (5 to 59 atoms, 1 to 4 binary or continuous probes each) in three runs with different input mixes: a bound moves 0.007 to 0.011 on average, 0.02 to 0.03 at the 95th percentile and 0.11 at most, and 1% to 4% of intervals move across 0.5. Novelty's CI callers pass no seed, so they keep seed 0; `--seed` does not change these intervals. Artifacts written before this change keep their old bounds; a re-run gives the new ones. Nothing in dokimi reads these bounds to decide a verdict.
