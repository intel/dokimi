### Fixed

- **Sign-flip interval and p of deltas near the float maximum** — the interval search of `repo compare` and the arms track (`sign_flip_interval`) emitted `RuntimeWarning: invalid value encountered in matmul` for deltas such as `[1e308, 0.0]`, and a `nan` flowed through its comparisons so the ends could be silently wrong. A row whose largest delta is above `max float / (8 * n)` is now searched on the deltas divided by a power of two (exact) and each end is multiplied back, so it neither warns nor leaves the float range. An end that comes back past the float maximum is the maximum, never `inf`. `sign_flip_p` divides the deltas by the same power of two, so its p moves to the exact value there (`sign_flip_p([1e308] * 3)` was 0.5, is now 0.25) and agrees with the interval. Rows below that threshold give the same p and ends as before.
