### Added

- Add `dokimi delivery release`, `dokimi delivery scan` and `dokimi delivery report` for immutable product deliveries. Release exits 0 for PASS, 1 for FAIL and 3 for INCONCLUSIVE. Scan writes paired observations in the `delivery.v1` format and exits 3 for partial coverage. A live scan makes paid model calls and records their usage. [Delivery contracts and scan replay](../docs/delivery.md) explains the inputs and example. Scan reports retain rejected success claims even when the response has no usable script. Scoring normalizes large finite group weights without overflow. A consumer that exits 125 or 137 is graded by the verifier instead of being counted as a runner failure. An inconclusive row from a failed consumer container keeps the script and captured output.
