### Fixed

- `catalog check --online` reads a `<...>` link destination up to its `>`: `[a](<https://acme.dev/x y>)` is requested as `https://acme.dev/x%20y` (it was cut at the space, so `/x` was probed), and a `|` inside an autolink or a `<...>` destination stays in the URL (`<https://acme.dev/a|b>` was cut at the pipe). In a table row (a line that starts with `|`) an unescaped pipe still ends the URL. A backslash-escaped `>` or `<` inside the `<...>` does not end it: `[a](<https://acme.dev/x y\>z>)` is requested as `https://acme.dev/x%20y%3Ez`. A `>` closes the destination only when a `)`, a title or the end of a definition's line follows it, so `[a](<https://acme.dev/a) is 3 > 2 today` is read as `https://acme.dev/a`, not as prose up to the `>`; and a `$` or `{` after a blank in a closed `<...>` destination is text, not a template marker.
