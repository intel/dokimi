### Fixed

- **Sign-flip interval of a Monte Carlo row whose deltas span more than a float sum resolves** — for a row of more than 16 changed deltas, `sign_flip_interval` counted each shift's resamples with float sums, so the small deltas of `[1e16] + [1.0] * 19` were absorbed by the large one and its lower end came out as 0.9474 (0.0001 for `1e20` or `1e300`) while the exact p puts it at 1.0. When the shifted deltas span more than 40 binary orders of magnitude, the resamples are now counted on exact integer sums, with the same draws, so `p(0)` still equals `sign_flip_p` for the seed. A row below that spread, and any row of 16 deltas or fewer, gives the same ends as before. The exact sums make such a row slower to search: about 0.2 s for `1e16` and about 15 s for `1e300`.
