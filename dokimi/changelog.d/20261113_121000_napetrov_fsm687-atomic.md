### Fixed

- **A failed write no longer leaves a half-written or stray file** — the pin, ledger, lock, atoms, probe, response-cache and task writers now publish through one helper: a pid- and thread-unique tmp file, then `os.replace`. A failed write keeps the file that was there and removes its tmp file.
