### Fixed

- **`dokimi skill novelty` no longer drops a valid reversal wording for being an antonym or quantifier inversion, or a tag question** — the reversal check accepts `always` against a source that holds `sometimes`, `occasionally`, `rarely`, `never` or `only the first` and `entirety` against one that holds `subset`, `partial`, `some` or `few`, when the candidate drops that limiter (`Only administrators ...` to `... always ...` widens nothing and is rejected), and `skip`, `unreliable`, `useless`, `unsuitable`, `unhelpful` and `wrong`; `skip` is cancelled only by a negative judgement that governs it (`wrong to skip`, `wrong to ever skip`, `skipping it is a mistake`), not one that describes what is skipped (`skip warm-up because it is useless`); a trailing `..., doesn't it?` or `..., is it not?` is question phrasing, not a negation. Each kind's `probes.by_kind.<kind>.rejected_examples` keeps the first 20 rejected wordings with their source and reasons (the `novelty.v1` schema caps it at 20 and requires `atom_id`, `source`, `candidate` and `reasons`), and the rejected-rate warning names it.
