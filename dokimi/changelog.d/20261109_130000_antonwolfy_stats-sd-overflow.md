### Fixed

- **`dokimi.eval.stats.paired_test` returns a result for finite deltas from 1e200 up to about 1e300** — the sample sd squared each deviation in plain floats, so a deviation above about 1.3e154 made it `inf` or raised `OverflowError`, out of `paired_test`, `arm_significance`, `mean_ci`, `point_biserial` and `dokimi.eval.interactions.interaction_test`. The sd now divides the deviations by the power of two at or below the largest one before squaring, and the t-test runs on the deltas divided the same way, so its p-value is the one the scaled-down deltas give. Near the float limit (about 1e307 and above) the mean, the deviations or the confidence interval still overflow: `mean_ci` and so `paired_test` raise `OverflowError` there rather than return an infinite interval. Judge scores of 0 to 100 never reached this. For them the division is exact and the p-values are unchanged; the sd now takes its square root with `math.sqrt`, which is correctly rounded, so it can differ from before in the last bit.
