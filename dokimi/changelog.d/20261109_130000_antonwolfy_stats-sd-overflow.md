### Fixed

- **`dokimi.eval.stats.paired_test` returns a result for finite deltas of 1e200 or more** — the sample sd squared each deviation in plain floats, so a deviation above about 1.3e154 made it `inf` or raised `OverflowError`, out of `paired_test`, `arm_significance`, `mean_ci`, `point_biserial` and `dokimi.eval.interactions.interaction_test`. The sd now divides the deviations by the power of two at or below the largest one before squaring, and the t-test runs on the deltas divided the same way, so its p-value is the one the scaled-down deltas give. Judge scores of 0 to 100 never reached this. For them the division is exact and the p-values are unchanged; the sd now takes its square root with `math.sqrt`, which is correctly rounded, so it can differ from before in the last bit.
