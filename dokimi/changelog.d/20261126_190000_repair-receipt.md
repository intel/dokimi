### Added

- **`catalog repair-receipt` lists what one edit did to the findings.** `catalog repair-receipt BEFORE AFTER EDITED` compares two saved `catalog check --format json` reports and prints the findings the edit cleared, added and retained, by whole row with line numbers left out, so a finding that only moved lines is retained (#960, #1315). It runs no scan of its own. `--online --fail-on-code` on `link-dead` and `package-not-found` is now covered by a test.
