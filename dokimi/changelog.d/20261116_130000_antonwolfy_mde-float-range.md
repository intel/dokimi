### Fixed

- **`mde` of deltas near the float maximum** — `sign_flip_summary` (`repo compare`) emitted `RuntimeWarning: overflow encountered in scalar multiply` computing `mde` for a spread near the float maximum (`[sys.float_info.max, 1, 1, 1, 1, 1]`), and `RuntimeWarning: overflow encountered in multiply` rounding it for twelve deltas of ±1e307. `min_detectable` now does its arithmetic on Python floats and `_round` converts to a float first, so neither warns. A planning figure beyond the float range, or any `inf` or `nan` passed to `_round`, is `None` (no figure to show) instead of `inf`. `min_detectable(sd, 0)` is still `nan`. The inconclusive note prints an `mde` of 1e6 or more in scientific notation (`about ±9.276e+306`) instead of every digit. The `mde` in the data and the note's text for an `mde` below 1e6 are unchanged.
