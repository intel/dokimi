## Statement
On 2,932 rows, judge generations within a family (openai, moonshot, anthropic, deepseek) are
compared:
- They agree at κ 0.61–0.72, about the same as judges from different vendors (mean κ
  0.51–0.64).
- Their lenient rates shift by up to ±0.12, with no common direction.
- One answer model's lenient added share moves by up to 0.15.
- Newer judges are mixed on mean κ against the other judges: it rises in five of seven old-to-new pairs and falls in both OpenAI pairs.

A three-family median panel cuts the generation shift to ≤0.07 lenient (≤0.11 strict) and
keeps the order of the extreme answer models on the lenient scale only; under strict, grok-4.6 passes nemotron-nano in the new panel.
Evidence for the generation run: docs/judge-panel-study.md section 6 and its generations/*.csv tables.

## Applies to
Judge panels, judge upgrades, longitudinal comparisons of graded results
