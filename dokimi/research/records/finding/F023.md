## Statement
Per-model added and known shares depend on the judge by up to 0.23 (lenient), and the
original study judge is at the lenient, low-added end. The order of answer models by the lenient
placebo-gated added share keeps nemotron first and opus last under all eight judges; the
models in between swap, and the ungated share does not keep opus last. Report shares with a
judge range, or a 3+ judge panel from different families, and keep claims about the extremes.

## Applies to
Skill novelty, arms reports
