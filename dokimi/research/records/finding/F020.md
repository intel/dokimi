## Statement
Per-model placebo-gated added shares move with the judge by up to 0.23 and known shares by up
to 0.32 (lenient, haiku-4.5), and the
original study judge is at the lenient, low-added end. The order of answer models by placebo-gated
added share keeps nemotron first and opus last under all eight judges (lenient); the middle
ranks change, and on the ungated added share the original judge puts haiku-4.5 below opus-5. Report shares with a judge range, or a 3+ judge
panel from different families, and keep relative claims.

## Applies to
Skill novelty, arms reports
