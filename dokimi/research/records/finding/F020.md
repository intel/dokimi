## Statement
On simulated judge deltas (35% ties), requiring both the paired t test and the Wilcoxon test below 0.05 gave
about 3% false positives at a nominal 5%, where one sign-flip test gives about 5%, and it found fewer real
shifts (0.52 against 0.63 at +4 over 80 questions). Its Student t interval contradicted its own verdict in up to
11% of runs. Moving the arms track to the sign-flip test changes verdicts near 0.05, almost always toward
significant: 2 of 418 changed verdicts went the other way.

## Applies to
`arms run` significance per arm and per interaction contrast, `report model-compare`, the with/without eval
report (`dokimi/eval/stats.py`); D018.
