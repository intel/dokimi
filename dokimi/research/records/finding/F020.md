## Statement
Per-model added and known shares depend on the judge by up to 0.23 (lenient), and the
original study judge is at the lenient, low-added end. The order of answer models by added
share keeps nemotron first and opus last under all eight judges (lenient). Report shares with a judge range, or a 3+ judge
panel from different families, and keep relative claims.

## Applies to
Skill novelty, arms reports
