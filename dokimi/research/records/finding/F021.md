## Statement
On the same verdicts, unanimous, mean-score and any-correct rules move the placebo-gated added
share by up to 0.20 against majority, because one rule applies to both the baseline and the
treatment arm. Majority, median and binary Dawid-Skene are equivalent here. Judges disagree
most on the no-skill and placebo arms (30% and 21% of rows not unanimous, against 17% with
the skill).

## Applies to
Judge panels, arms run, eval judge agreement
