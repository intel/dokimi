## Statement
On the same verdicts, the any-correct rule moves the lenient placebo-gated added share by up to
0.20 against majority (mean-score up to 0.11, unanimous up to 0.08), because one rule applies to
both the baseline and the treatment arm. Majority and binary Dawid-Skene differ by at most 0.016
on the gated added share and 0.033 on the known share, and agree on 99.3% of rows. Judges disagree
most on the no-skill and placebo arms (30% and 21% of rows not unanimous, against 17% with
the skill).

## Applies to
Judge panels, arms run, eval judge agreement
