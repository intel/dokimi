## Setup
Same answers judged by Haiku, gpt-oss, Sonnet, Opus; absolute vs pairwise; sequential (SPRT) gate.

## Result
Verdict sign flipped with judge (Haiku -3.4 p=.024; Opus +1.2 p=.005). Per-question direction agreement 46-76%. Only one absolute judge passed the sequential gate; pairwise gpt-oss passed at 0.962 for ~$0.5. Several earlier claims retracted after a pair-keying audit.

- local run, ~11k judge calls
