## Setup
Same answers judged by Haiku, gpt-oss, Sonnet, Opus; absolute vs pairwise; sequential (SPRT) gate.

## Result
Verdict sign flipped with judge (Haiku -3.4 p=.024; Opus +1.2 p=.005). Per-question direction agreement 46-76%. Only one absolute judge passed the sequential gate; pairwise gpt-oss passed at 0.962 for ~$0.5. 
## Corrections
A pair-keying audit retracted three later claims: that every absolute judge, Opus included, failed the gate; that margin cascades never pass strip_code; and that Haiku pairwise prefers the contradicting answer 84% of the time. The sign flip above was not retracted.

## Evidence
- local run, ~11k judge calls
