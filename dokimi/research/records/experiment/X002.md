## Setup
Compare judge pairwise accuracy on degradations to a predictor using answer length only.

## Result
Defect battery 0.94 vs length-only 0.81 on 5 variants; length beat the battery on strip_code and truncate. Shipped hosted judge 9-variant: 0.920 vs length 0.663; 4 of 9 variants <= length.

## Evidence
- local run, Haiku judge, 20 bases, 176 pairs
