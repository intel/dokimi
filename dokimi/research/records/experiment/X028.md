## Setup
Haiku judge, 4 seeds; criterion plus an instruction to enumerate every figure, against the baseline prompt.

## Result
Pooled 0.599 -> 0.712, p=0.0144 over 156 pairs. Still under the 0.80 gate, so the judge is better but not reliable on numbers (see F003).

## Evidence
- local run, Haiku, 156 pairs, 4 seeds
