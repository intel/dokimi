## Setup
One arm against its baseline, before any multiplicity correction, 1000 simulated runs per row. Each per-question
delta is a tie (0) with probability 0.35; otherwise it is drawn from N(shift, 12) and rounded to 0.1, or, for the
heavy-tailed rows, 12 times a t variable with 2 degrees of freedom plus the shift. Each run is judged two ways:
the arms track's rule at the time ("today": t test p and Wilcoxon p both below 0.05) and the repo track's
sign-flip test (exact p below 0.05, with the floor 2/2^k over the k changed pairs). The last column counts the
runs where the t interval excludes 0 and the t-and-Wilcoxon verdict says not significant, or the reverse.

It was run once, before the arms track changed (issue #421), on `main` e893574, with the repo track's own
`sign_flip_p`, `min_sign_flip_p` and `sign_flip_interval`; it was not rerun when the change landed.

## Result
| deltas | questions | significant today | significant by sign-flip | only sign-flip | only today | today's t interval disagrees with today's verdict |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| null | 12 | 0.038 | 0.044 | 7 | 1 | 0.012 |
| null | 30 | 0.029 | 0.053 | 24 | 0 | 0.024 |
| null | 80 | 0.030 | 0.054 | 24 | 0 | 0.025 |
| shift +4 | 12 | 0.092 | 0.121 | 29 | 0 | 0.028 |
| shift +4 | 30 | 0.219 | 0.295 | 77 | 1 | 0.075 |
| shift +4 | 80 | 0.522 | 0.634 | 112 | 0 | 0.114 |
| shift +4, heavy tails | 12 | 0.049 | 0.078 | 29 | 0 | 0.008 |
| shift +4, heavy tails | 30 | 0.090 | 0.141 | 51 | 0 | 0.020 |
| shift +4, heavy tails | 80 | 0.176 | 0.239 | 63 | 0 | 0.031 |

Under the null the old rule's false-positive rate is about 3% at a nominal 5%; the sign-flip test's is about 5%.
Of 418 changed verdicts, 2 went from significant to not significant; the rest became significant.
