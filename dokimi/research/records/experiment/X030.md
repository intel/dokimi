## Setup
2,664 existing study answers (444 probe-model cells, all six arms, five answer models,
stratified by skill and reversal probe) plus 268 fresh no-skill repeats, re-graded with the
study's prompt by seven judges from seven families. The original judge's grades were kept.
The aggregation rules compared were majority, median, mean, unanimous, any-correct and
Dawid-Skene, over panel sizes 1-7.

## Result
The lenient placebo-gated added share moves by 0.05-0.23 per answer model across judges, and
the original judge is the most lenient, at the low end. The ranking of answer models is stable
under every judge. Pairwise lenient kappa is 0.70-0.88, and highest within one family. No
self-preference above 0.04 was seen. Majority, median and Dawid-Skene agree on 99.3% of rows.
The rule moves numbers as much as the judge does. Three judges cut the spread from 0.039 to
0.025 SD. Details: docs/judge-panel-study.md.

## Evidence
- local run, 2,932 answers x 7 judges, 20,524 grading calls
