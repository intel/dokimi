## Setup
Local 4B/14B model, deterministic degradations of perf golden answers; positive vs negative wording of the same decision.

## Result
Positive 'covers point?' gave P(yes)=1.000 for intact and degraded copies; negative 'is it absent?' separated them (0.825 vs 0.250). Negative wording then saturated at the floor on a less acquiescent 14B model.

## Evidence
- local run, Qwen3 4B/14B, 20 bases
