## What
The arms track takes D018's method: `dokimi/eval/sign_flip.py` holds the sign-flip test and its inverted interval,
which `repo compare` and `dokimi/eval/stats.py` both import. Every paired arms result (`arms run` per arm and per
interaction contrast, `report model-compare`, the with/without eval report) reports the sign-flip `p`, `n_changed`
and the interval with `ci_method` `sign_flip_inversion`; `p_ttest`, `p_wilcoxon` and `n_wilcoxon` are gone from new
records, and `arms.v1.json` is widened in place so older records still validate.
