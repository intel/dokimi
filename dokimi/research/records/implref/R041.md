## What
`extract_atoms` returns every extracted atom and `sample_atoms` picks the cap's spread sample. Spans, attribution, sole carriers and shortening's structure index all of them, so `frac_carrying_nothing` is the artifact's and `spans.capped_atom_set` is false on a capped run; the paid stages and the shortening oracle stay on the sample. `atoms.json` holds every atom and names the sample in `sampled_atom_ids`, which `novelty probe` and `novelty shorten` measure by default and `--all-atoms` widens to every atom.
